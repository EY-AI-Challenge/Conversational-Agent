# Strategic Pitch

## Problem

EY knowledge is valuable but fragmented across partner CVs, service-line spreadsheets, internal transcripts, reports, and public-facing articles. Consultants lose time searching, validating, and turning this knowledge into usable client material.

## Consequence

Fragmented knowledge creates operational friction:

- slower onboarding;
- repeated research work;
- harder access to internal expertise;
- longer preparation cycles before client conversations;
- lower reuse of institutional knowledge.

## Solution

Knowledge Navigator EY Assistant is a cited conversational assistant over curated EY knowledge. A consultant can ask a question in natural language and receive a grounded answer with supporting sources.

The solution is not just a chatbot. It is a knowledge mobilization layer that helps professionals move from "where is the information?" to "how can I use it in this client context?".

## Demo Story

The live demo should show a consultant preparing for a client meeting:

1. Ask who is responsible for a service line.
2. Ask for relevant partner experience.
3. Ask for a short client briefing about AI or transformation.
4. Show source citations.
5. Upload a new CV/document and ask a follow-up question.

## Business Impact

- Faster knowledge retrieval.
- Better reuse of partner expertise.
- Shorter project preparation time.
- Improved onboarding for new joiners.
- More consistent and auditable answers through source attribution.

## Roadmap

- Expand curated source coverage.
- Add enterprise authentication and access control.
- Integrate with internal knowledge systems.
- Add feedback loops and answer quality evaluation.
- Add monitoring, usage analytics, and governance dashboards.
